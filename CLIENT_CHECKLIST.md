# Client Onboarding Checklist: Luxury Makeup Academy & Bridal Studio

Use this checklist when pitching this website template or onboarding a new makeup academy or bridal studio client. Send this document to the business owner so they can provide all required assets in one organized batch.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile & Reviews
- [ ] **Google Maps Listing Link**: Send your public Google Maps link (e.g. `https://maps.google.com/?q=...`).
- [ ] **Google Place ID** (or studio address exactly as it appears on Google): Enables live synchronization of your 5-star ratings, verified bride reviews, and Google Maps photos.

---

## 2. Google Drive Portfolio Media (Photos & Certificates)
- [ ] **Google Drive Folder**: Create a Google Drive folder containing your best high-resolution work:
  - Real Brides (Royal Red, Pastel, South Indian, Reception)
  - Pre-wedding & Engagement looks
  - Haldi & Mehendi looks
  - Saree draping & intricate hairstyles
  - Before / After transformation photos (high-res bare face + finished makeup)
  - Academy classroom training photos & student graduation moments
  - Scans or photos of your Certificates & Diplomas
- [ ] **CRITICAL PERMISSION SETTING**:
  - Right-click the folder or individual files in Google Drive.
  - Select **Share** > **General access**.
  - Change to: **"Anyone with the link"** and role set to **"Viewer"**.
  - *Note:* The template automatically converts these share links into high-speed, high-resolution direct thumbnails!

---

## 3. YouTube Videos & Reels
- [ ] **YouTube Links (3 to 6 videos)**:
  - YouTube watch links, youtu.be links, or YouTube Shorts URLs.
  - Great examples: Bridal transformations, Airbrush demos, Masterclass teasers, Student testimonials.
  - *The template automatically grabs the HD thumbnail and plays in a fast, privacy-friendly popup player.*

---

## 4. WhatsApp, Phone & Studio Contact Details
- [ ] **Primary WhatsApp Business Number**: With country code (e.g. `919876543210` for India).
- [ ] **Calling Number**: For direct phone calls on mobile.
- [ ] **Studio Physical Address**: Full address with floor, building, landmark, and pincode.
- [ ] **Working Hours**: E.g. `Mon - Sun: 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM (By Appointment Only)`.
- [ ] **Instagram Handle**: E.g. `@yourstudio_makeup`.

---

## 5. Services & Bridal Packages
- [ ] **Bridal & Event Services List**:
  - HD Bridal Makeup
  - Airbrush Makeup
  - Engagement / Sangeet / Reception Looks
  - Saree Draping & Hair Extensions
  - Destination Wedding Services
- [ ] **3 Bridal Package Tiers**:
  1. *Classic / Elegance Tier* (Included services + Starting price)
  2. *Signature Luxury HD Tier* (Most popular package + Price)
  3. *Royal / Destination Master Tier* (Pricing or "Price on Request")

---

## 6. Academy Courses & Upcoming Batches
- [ ] **Course Names & Durations**:
  - E.g. 6-Week Master Diploma, 2-Week Airbrush Masterclass, 3-Day Personal Grooming.
- [ ] **Course Inclusions**:
  - Professional Vanity Kit details, ISO Certificate, model practice, placement support.
- [ ] **Batch Start Dates & Fees**:
  - Start dates (e.g., 1st & 15th of each month) and tuition fees/EMI options.

---

## 7. Founder Bio, Achievements & Accreditations
- [ ] **Founder / Master Artist Name & Title**: E.g., "Aanya Sharma - Celebrity Bridal Artist & Educator".
- [ ] **High-Resolution Portrait Photo**: For the "Meet the Artist" section.
- [ ] **Years of Experience & Bride Count**: E.g. 12+ years, 1,500+ brides, 850+ academy graduates.
- [ ] **Key Industry Awards & Milestones**: Best Bridal Studio awards, fashion week credits, magazine features.
- [ ] **Pro Products Used**: MAC Pro, Bobbi Brown, Dior, Charlotte Tilbury, Temptu Airbrush, Huda Beauty.

---

## How to Customize in 5 Minutes:
1. Open `index.html` in any text editor.
2. Scroll to `<script id="client-config">`.
3. Fill in the values in the `CONFIG` object labeled with `// REPLACE`.
4. Save and host on any static hosting (Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages, Hostinger, cPanel, or Cloudflare Pages) with zero build steps!
